                          How to use PondWise

                          WHAT is this?

                          PondWise is your fishpond notebook on the phone or computer. It remembers your ponds, fish, food, tasks, and money.

                          WHY use it?

                          So you do not forget feeding, you spot sick water early, and you know if you earned or lost money.

                          WHERE is what?

                          Ponds = your ponds. Feed = food calculator. Tasks = things to do. Records = daily notes. Money = money in and out. Helper (here) = ask questions.

                          HOW do I start? (4 steps)

                          1. Go to Ponds. Add your pond. Tap its card to pick it.
                          2. Write your fish: name, how many, how big.
                          3. Every day: open Records, save food + water. Open Tasks, tick done.
                          4. When you pay or sell: open Money, save it. Profit shows on top.

                          Backup

                          Save a copy of your notes. Keep it safe.
